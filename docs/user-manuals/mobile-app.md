# About this manual

This manual is for residents of Echague who use the SERBIS app to ask the MDRRMO for services, book patient transport, borrow equipment and read safety guides.

SERBIS is for non-emergency requests. **In an emergency, call the hotline.** The numbers are in the app's Safety library.

Each task has numbered steps. Red numbered boxes on a picture match the numbers in the legend under it.

**Account types.** What you can request depends on your account:

- **Head of the Family**: for a household.
- **Barangay**: for a barangay hall. MDRRMO creates these accounts.
- **Organization**: for a school, company or group. It can request services only after MDRRMO approves it.

Some services are only for certain account types. For example, relief goods are for barangay accounts, and trainings and drills are for barangays and organizations.

# Get started

## Install the app

SERBIS is not on the Play Store. Organizations wait for MDRRMO to activate the account before filing requests.

1. Open https://serbis-landing-page.vercel.app/ on your Android phone.
2. Tap **Download APK** and wait for the file to finish.
3. Open the file from your notifications or Downloads.
4. If the phone asks, allow installs from this source.
5. Open SERBIS and create your account.

1. Tap **Register**.
2. Under **Registering as**, choose **Head of the Family** or **Organization**.
3. Type your name and mobile number. An organization also types its name, and the name of its contact person.
4. Tap **Next: Where you live**.

SERBIS texts a 6-digit code to your number. The screen is called **Check your messages**.

1. Type the code.
2. Tap **Verify**.

If the text does not come, wait for **Resend code in …** to count down 60 seconds, then tap **Send a new code**. If you typed the wrong number, tap **Back to log in** and register again.

# Book an ambulance

1. Tap **Ambulance**.

# Follow your requests

## See all your requests

1. Tap **Track**.
2. Open requests are under **In progress**. Finished ones are under **Past requests**.

![MOB-24](screenshots/raw/MOB-24.png)

> **1** Requests still open
> **2** Where it stands
> **3** When it last changed

Each request shows one status:

- **Under review**: the MDRRMO is checking it.
- **Booked**: an ambulance booking holding its date and time. The MDRRMO assigns a unit before the trip.
- **Responding**: the team is working on it.
- **Approved**: for trainings, drills and certifications.
- **Completed**: done.
- **Not transported**: the ambulance trip did not carry the patient. The reason is shown.
- **Not approved**: the MDRRMO could not approve it. The reason is shown.
- **Cancelled by you**: you withdrew it.

# Common problems

**You are offline.** A bar at the top says **No connection to MDRRMO. Requests can't be sent.** and shows when the data was last updated. You can read saved requests and guides, but you cannot send anything until you are back online.

![MOB-38](screenshots/raw/MOB-38.png)

> **1** You are offline
> **2** How old the shown data is

**A list could not load.** Check your connection, then tap **Try again**.

![MOB-39](screenshots/raw/MOB-39.png)

> **1** What went wrong
> **2** Load it again

**The text-message code did not arrive.** Wait for the countdown to reach 0, then tap **Send a new code**. Check that the number is right.

**A service is not on the list.** It is not offered for your account type, or the MDRRMO has turned it off.

**"Your organization account is awaiting MDRRMO approval."** You can request services after the MDRRMO activates your account.

**"This account has been deactivated. Please visit the MDRRMO office."** Visit or call the MDRRMO.

**The app asks you to update.** Install the newest version of the app, then open it again.
