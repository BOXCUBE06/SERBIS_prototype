## Why the raw figure was 72.09%

Query (a) run on 2026-10-18 counted the eligible residents as they are now, not as they were at send time:

- Raw: eligible 43, reached 31, 31 / 43 = 72.09%.
- 12 of the 43 were created on Oct 15 and activated after both blasts (bulk Activate test, about 14:30 to 14:33 UTC). They were Pending at send time, so the blast correctly skipped them. Their phone numbers were valid.
- Evidence: all 12 have `updated_at` after 12:28:11 UTC (`changed_after_blast1 = 12`, `changed_after_blast2 = 12`).
- Eligible at send time: 43 - 12 = 31. Targeting: 31 / 31 = 100%.

**Caveat:** `updated_at` changes on any edit, so this is strong evidence, not proof, that the 12 were activated after the blasts. The runbook already warns that residents changed after a send move the figure without the blast being wrong; run query (a) right after a send to avoid this.
